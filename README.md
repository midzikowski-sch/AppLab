# AppLab
Community platform for AI app builders
## Bio
AppLab is a thinktank community open to anyone who's interested in building apps with AI/vibecoding. We brainstorm project ideas, and share the best tools to use to bring them to life. We also host meetups and events, scheduled by members in our Discord community. Official course materials can also be found on our website.
